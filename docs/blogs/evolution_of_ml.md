# Evolution of Machine Learning Life Cycle

In this blog post on Substack, I discuss how the Machine Learning Development Life Cycle (MLDLC) has evolved over the years, from simple script-based models to complex, automated MLOps pipelines.

I explore the shifts in:
*   Data Engineering patterns
*   Model Training and Tracking
*   Deployment strategies
*   The rise of GenAI and Agentic workflows
